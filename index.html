About Naebi

Accountable levy administration, built for aviation

Naebi Dynamic Concepts Ltd manages the collection of levies on civilian helicopter movement, giving operators a clear, dependable process and giving regulators an accurate, auditable record.

We combine financial discipline with a working knowledge of aviation operations, so every movement is recorded correctly and every payment is accounted for.

“Our aim is to be a first-rate global leader in aviation levy administration.”

What we stand on

Our pillars

Financial Integrity

Every levy is recorded, reconciled and reported. Operators and regulators can trace each payment from the movement it covers to the account it reaches.

Security & Safety

Payment data and operator records are handled with strict access controls. We work alongside aviation safety standards, never around them.

Customer Support

A responsive team answers operator questions quickly. Clearance queries, payment confirmations and records are handled by people who know the process.

Regulatory Compliance

Our processes are aligned with Nigeria's aviation authorities, so operators meet their obligations with a single, compliant payment.

Operational Excellence

From movement to clearance, without the friction

Each helicopter movement is logged, the applicable levy is assessed and the operator is notified. Once payment is confirmed, clearance is issued and the record is closed. It is a simple process, run with care at every step.

Levy and clearance questions

The levy is assessed per helicopter movement according to the approved schedule. Contact our team for the current schedule that applies to your operation.

The levy applies to civilian helicopter movements covered by the applicable regulations. If you are unsure whether your operation is covered, our team will confirm it for you.

Use the Make Payment option on this site, or contact either of our offices for the accepted payment channels and account details.
